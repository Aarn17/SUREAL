The Story
Born from the streets,
made for the bold.

It started with a pair of jeans, a bag of rhinestones, and an obsession with making something no one else had. What began as a personal project — decorating pieces for friends — grew into a movement.

SUREAL was founded on a simple belief: that clothing should be an extension of who you are — not what a trend tells you to be. Since 2018, we've handcrafted over 500 one-of-a-kind pieces, each unique, each personal, each real.

What We Stand For

VALUES

01

Our Journey

TIMELINE

2018
The First Piece

A single pair of jeans, decorated with rhinestones in a bedroom. The first SUREAL piece — no brand, just craft. Given to a friend. The reaction was everything.

●
●
2020
Word Spreads

Without advertising, demand grew through word of mouth. Custom orders started flooding in. The brand was born — not by marketing, but by quality that spoke for itself.

2022
The Studio Opens

A dedicated space to create. More materials, more techniques — hand-painting, embroidery, screen-print. SUREAL expanded beyond denim into a full streetwear vision.

●
●
2024
Now

500+ pieces made. A global community of wearers who chose to be different. An online store launching to bring SUREAL to the world. The movement is real.
